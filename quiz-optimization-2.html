Default setup

The standard setup of the Optimization 2 assignment: Llama-style architecture with 8 layers, hidden size 512, 8 attention heads, QK-norm, a 4k vocab size tokenizer, and about 35M parameters (d8); 614.4M training tokens (9,375 updates) of fresh data; AdamW with $\beta_1, \beta_2 = (0.9, 0.95)$ and $\epsilon = 10^{-8}$, linear decay learning rate schedule with 1% linear warmup (93 updates), peak learning rate .003, context length 1024, batch size 64, weight decay 0.1 (masked: hidden and readout matrices decay; embeddings and normalization gains do not), and 1.0 grad clipping; model and data seed 42. Lower validation loss is better. Seed-to-seed noise in the final validation loss is about .003.

Conventions

We follow the same conventions as the rest of A3.

  • wsdX schedule: after the warmup, the peak LR is held constant and then decays linearly to zero over the final fraction $X$ of training (wsd0.2 decays from update 7,500).
  • Regret (Problem 2(c)): a run’s validation loss minus the control run’s at the same update.
  • Sharpness: $\lambda_{\max}$ is the top eigenvalue of the loss Hessian, measured in FP32 on the 64-sequence held-out probe. A family block is the Hessian restricted to one parameter family (embedding, attention, MLP, RMSNorm gains, LM head), as in Problem 4(a).
  • Full-batch runs (Problem 3(a)): plain gradient descent on the first $N$ training sequences, embedding initialized $16\times$ larger, no clipping, no weight decay, constant LR $\eta$.
